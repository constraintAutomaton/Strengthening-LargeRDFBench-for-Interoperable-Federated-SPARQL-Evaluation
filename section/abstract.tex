\begin{abstract}
	% Context
	LargeRDFBench is one of the most comprehensive benchmarks for evaluating federated SPARQL
	query engines, combining a large collection of real, interlinked datasets with a rich query
	suite that has made it a reference point for the community.
	Its value rests on the same
	promise as RDF itself: data published under shared specifications can be exchanged and
	queried by any conformant tool.
	% Need
	As the benchmark continues to be widely adopted and the range of conformant engines grows,
	the interoperability of its artifacts becomes ever more important. Several of its data dumps
	diverge from strict specification conformance, and its expected query results are distributed
	in an ad-hoc format -- which can prevent standards-compliant engines from loading the data
	and make results difficult to verify automatically.
	% Task
	We systematically identify and categorize these data-quality issues and repair them,
	producing standards-conformant serializations of every affected dataset, and we re-encode the
	benchmark's expected results in the W3C SPARQL Results JSON format.
	% Object
	We contribute a polished, standards-compliant edition of LargeRDFBench, produced by a
	reproducible cleaning pipeline, together with its expected query results in a standard,
	machine-verifiable format.
	% Findings
	Every dataset now parses under strict, specification-compliant RDF parsers and the expected
	results are machine-verifiable through a standard format, extending the benchmark's reach to
	the full range of conformant engines while preserving its original content.
	% Conclusion
	This work strengthens an already valuable community resource: by aligning its artifacts with
	the very specifications the systems under test are expected to honor, we broaden the set of
	engines that can be fairly and reproducibly compared, and reaffirm standards compliance as a
	foundation for trustworthy benchmarking.
\end{abstract}
